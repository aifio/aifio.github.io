Privacy Policy

Application: Windows 11 New Tab (browser extension) · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Windows 11 New Tab browser extension ("the extension", "we", "our") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Our home page is https://aifio.ir/win11-newtab/. The extension replaces your browser's new tab page with a customizable Windows 11-style desktop.

1. Summary

  • Your shortcuts, settings, notes and preferences are stored locally in your browser.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your personal data, and we do not sell or share your data.
  • Signing in with Google is optional and only used to show your account name and to back up/restore your own extension data to a private Google Drive app-data folder.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.

2. Information stored on your device

The extension uses the browser's extension storage (storage) to save: desktop icons and folders, favorite websites, theme and wallpaper settings, widget and window layout, notes and to-do items, reading list items, pet settings, and optional API keys that you type in yourself (for example for a wallpaper provider). This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up (see sections 3 and 5). Uninstalling the extension or clearing its data removes it.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose "Sign in with Google" / "Connect Google", the extension uses Google OAuth 2.0 and requests these scopes:

ScopeWhy it is requestedHow it is used
userinfo.email, userinfo.profileTo identify the connected account.Your name, email address and profile picture are displayed inside the extension's account section. They are kept locally in your browser and are not sent to us or to anyone else.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdataTo back up and restore your extension data.The extension creates and reads a file in the hidden Google Drive "Application Data" folder. This folder is only accessible to this extension; the extension cannot view, list or modify any of your other Drive files. The file contains the same data described in section 2.

Limited Use. The extension's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data: (a) is used only to provide and improve the user-facing backup and account features described here; (b) is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide those features or to comply with law; (c) is not used for serving advertisements; (d) is not read by humans, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or to comply with law; and (e) is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

The extension does not use Google APIs to generate, store or process images or other content, and it does not use Google APIs for AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII) or any content that violates the Google APIs Terms of Service.

You can disconnect your account at any time from the extension's account settings, and you can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the backup by removing the app's data from your Google Drive settings (Drive → Settings → Manage apps).

5. Web toolbar and website access

The extension runs a small floating toolbar ("Web Dock") on web pages and requests broad site access so it can show this toolbar, add the current site to your desktop, and take screenshots or screen recordings when you click those buttons. The extension does not read, collect, store or transmit the content of the pages you visit, your browsing history, passwords or form data. Screenshots and recordings are created on your device and are saved only where you choose.

6. Permissions

  • storage, unlimitedStorage – save your settings and data locally.
  • favicon – show website icons.
  • identity, identity.email – optional Google sign-in.
  • declarativeNetRequest – let selected sites open inside the extension's in-page windows.
  • Host access (all URLs) – show the Web Dock toolbar and load wallpapers, icons and the services listed in section 4.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal data. We have no advertising or analytics partners.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it, clear the extension's data, or uninstall the extension. Backup data in your Google Drive app-data folder stays until you delete it or revoke the extension's access. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, there is nothing further for us to delete; you can also email us with any request.

9. Security

All communication with Google and other services uses HTTPS. Google access tokens are kept in the extension's local storage and are used only to call the Google APIs listed above. Please keep your browser profile and Google account secure.

10. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes to how Google user data is used will be announced in the extension or on our home page.
