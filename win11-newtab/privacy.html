Privacy Policy for Windows 11 New Tab

1. Data Collection & Storage
The "Windows 11 New Tab" extension respects your privacy. All your customized settings are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services
To provide dynamic features, this extension temporarily connects to third-party APIs:

  • Open-Meteo: To fetch local weather data.
  • Unsplash / Wallhaven: To fetch background wallpapers.
These secure services may receive standard network requests (including your IP address) strictly to provide the requested content.

3. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback, please contact the developer via our GitHub repository.
